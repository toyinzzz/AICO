## Competitive Landscape

### Major Competitors & Pricing

#### Optimizely
- **Pricing**: $1,440/month (annual) or $49 per 1,000 monthly visitors
- **Position**: Enterprise-focused, most expensive option
- **Strengths**: Brand recognition, comprehensive features
- **Weakness**: High cost, complex setup

#### VWO (Visual Website Optimizer)
- **Pricing**: 
  - Testing: $314-1,265/month (annual)
  - Growth plan: $299/month (100K tested users)
  - Free plan: <10,000 monthly tested users
- **Position**: Mid-market alternative to Optimizely
- **Strengths**: Better pricing, comprehensive CRO suite
- **Weakness**: Not profit-focused

#### Market Gaps Identified
1. **No Profit-First Approach**: All competitors focus on conversion rates, not profit optimization
2. **Complex Pricing**: Most tools have complex visitor-based pricing
3. **Feature Overload**: Enterprise tools too complex for SMBs
4. **Limited AI Integration**: Basic AI features, not profit-optimized
